# 🚍 LlegoYa

## Project Overview

LlegoYa is a smart urban mobility platform designed to improve the user experience of public transportation in Medellín.

Currently, many people do not know which route to take, how long a bus will take to arrive, or what the best alternative is to reach their destination. This lack of information leads to unnecessary waiting times, uncertainty, and a poor overall commuting experience.

LlegoYa aims to solve this problem through a combination of:

- Real-time geolocation.
- Transit route visualization.
- Live bus tracking.
- Artificial Intelligence for route searching.
- Centralized management of routes, drivers, and vehicles.

The platform will allow both users and transport companies to interact with a single, updated, and reliable source of information.

---

# Problem Statement

Currently, commuters face challenges such as:

- Lack of knowledge regarding available routes near their destinations.
- Absence of information on nearby buses operating across the city.
- Difficulty finding the best combination of routes.
- Unpredictable waiting times (no certainty of when a bus will arrive).
- Poor digitalization of transit information.

On the other hand, transport companies often lack modern tools to:

- Monitor their vehicles.
- Manage drivers.
- Manage their fleet.
- Visualize active routes.
- Analyze operational data.

LlegoYa seeks to bridge this gap through a modern technological solution based on data and geolocation.

# Project Objectives

## Main Objective

To build an intelligent platform capable of facilitating urban mobility through real-time transit information and artificial intelligence-driven assistance.

## Specific Objectives

- Enable users to look up public transportation routes.
- Display buses in real time on an interactive map.
- Recommend routes using AI.
- Record search and query history.
- Allow users to save favorite routes.
- Facilitate driver and vehicle management.
- Centralize the operational information of transport companies.

# Target Audience

The platform is designed for three main groups:

## Medellín Public Transit Users

People who use urban routes to commute within Medellín.

Needs:

- Find routes.
- Know where the bus is.
- Reduce waiting times.
- Get recommendations.

---

## Drivers

Personnel responsible for operating the transit vehicles.

Needs:

- Share their real-time location with users.
- Consult assigned routes through the application.
- Keep their operational status updated.

---

## Administrators

Personnel from the transport companies.

Needs:

- Manage drivers.
- Manage buses.
- Create and update routes.
- Supervise the operation.

# MVP Scope

The first version of the project will include:

- Authentication.
- Interactive map.
- Bus tracking.
- Smart chat.
- Route management.
- Bus management.
- Driver management.

The following will not be included initially:

- Payment system.
- Marketplace.
- Advanced analytics.
- Advanced multi-company management.
- Global super-administrator.

These features may be implemented in future versions.
