# Product Vision

## Our Vision

To become the benchmark platform for smart mobility in Medellín, providing reliable, accessible, and real-time information to improve the transportation experience for thousands of users.

LlegoYa does not aim to be just a map or a bus tracker.

Our vision is to build an intelligent ecosystem capable of understanding the mobility context of users and helping them make better travel decisions according to their needs.

---

# What We Code to Build

We want to develop a platform capable of answering questions such as:

- Which route works for me to get to my destination?
- Which bus is closest?
- How long do I have to wait?
- What is the best transportation alternative?
- Which routes pass through this area?

The answers to these questions must be based on real and updated data.

---

# Why It Matters

Urban mobility is one of the main challenges in large cities.

Thousands of people lose time daily due to:

- Scattered information.
- Lack of updated data.
- Manual processes.
- Absence of modern technological tools.

LlegoYa seeks to reduce these difficulties through an accessible solution for any user.

---

# Technological Vision

The platform will be built following modern software engineering principles:

- Scalable architecture.
- Distributed systems.
- Hybrid databases.
- Contextual artificial intelligence.
- Real-time communication.

The solution must be capable of evolving from an MVP into a large-scale enterprise platform.

---

# Artificial Intelligence Vision

LlegoYa's AI will not be a generic chatbot.

It will be a specialized assistant in urban mobility.

It must:

- Understand routes.
- Understand locations.
- Interpret natural language questions.
- Recommend transportation options.
- Use up-to-date information.
- Avoid fabricated responses (hallucinations).

In the long term, it will be able to train on historical data to offer increasingly accurate recommendations.

---

# Future Vision

Future versions could include:

- Arrival time prediction.
- Smart alerts.
- Integration with fare collection systems.
- Operational analysis for transit companies.
- Automatic route optimization.
- Traffic congestion prediction.
- Full mobile application.
- Multimodal AI.

Our goal is to build a solid foundation that allows us to evolve toward these capabilities without needing to rebuild the core architecture.
